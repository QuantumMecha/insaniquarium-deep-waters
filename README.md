# Insaniquarium: Deep Waters

A bigger, meaner fan remake of PopCap's Insaniquarium. Runs in the browser, no install.

**Play:** open `index.html` in Chrome or Firefox. Progress saves automatically to the browser,
including mid-level. Close the tab anytime and hit **Continue** later.

## Controls
- Click water: drop food ($5). Click coins to collect.
- Click aliens to zap them. Click missiles and leeches to pop them.
- Number keys 1-9 buy from the shop bar. Esc or P pauses.

## What's new vs. the original
- **14 fish** with a real food chain: Breeder → baby guppies → Carnivore → Megachomp,
  large guppies → stars → Starcatcher, Moon Jellies → Sea Turtle.
- **Fish interactions:** Seahorse love hearts double coin drops, Volt Eels zap fish into
  dropping coins, Piranhas swarm aliens but eat guppies when starving, Wrasses clean poop
  and pluck leeches, Puffers puff up and hurt aliens, Anglers collect coins and reveal ghosts,
  Golden Koi grant wishes.
- **Tank hygiene:** from Tank 2, fish poop. Dirty tanks make fish hungry and lazy.
- **12 aliens / 4 bosses:** Kraken, Leech Queen, Void Maw, the Overlord, plus twins that revive
  each other, a colour-switching squid, an invisible Phantom, a money-stealing Hoarder…
- **Bounties** from Captain Barnacle, **random events** (Gold Rush, Blackout, Meteor Shower,
  Treasure Current, Red Tide…), 3 **objectives** per level.
- **13 pets**, a **Shell Shop** of permanent upgrades, **22 trophies**, **Endless mode**, and a Fishopedia.

## Roguelike layer
- **Pet combos:** 15 pet pairs unlock special effects (Inky + Zippy = *Ink Storm*, Sluggo + Midas =
  *Gold Digger*…). Each pet belongs to a family (Collector, Guardian, Nurturer, Treasure); 3 of a family
  triggers a **transformation** (e.g. *Strike Team*: pets deal double damage, aliens slow down).
- **Relics:** beating each tank boss lets you pick 1 of 3 permanent relics. Equip 3 at a time.
- **Expedition mode** (unlocks after level 3): 16 random stages across 4 tanks. After each stage draft
  a boon (some cursed: big upside, real downside) or a pet; draft a pet twice to level it up (★★★).
  See the next stage's threats before you pick. Lose once and the run ends; relics and sand dollars stay.
